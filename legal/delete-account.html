Delete your Greenmove account

This page explains how to ask for your Greenmove Visits account and its data to be deleted. Greenmove is operated by Heal Line L.L.C-FZ, United Arab Emirates.

In the app

Sign in, open My account, tap Delete My Account, type DELETE and submit. The app shows the date by which deletion will be complete.

Without the app

Email helsayed@heal-line.com from the email address of your Greenmove account with the subject "Delete my Greenmove account". We may ask you to confirm that the address is yours before acting on the request.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in, your profile (name, company, role, specialty, city), profile photo, business card or ID image, vehicle details and photo, your visit requests and their history, your messages with hospitals, and your notifications.

What may be kept, and for how long

Operational audit records that do not contain your documents are removed on a fixed schedule, within 180 days. Backups are used only for recovery, expire on a fixed schedule, and completed deletions are applied again if a backup is restored. Records we are legally required to keep are retained only as long as the law requires. Store purchase records are held by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Timing

Requests are processed within 30 days and confirmed by email.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Greenmove account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google Play. Cancel it in your Apple subscription settings or in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions before deleting the account.
