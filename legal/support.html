Support

For account, visit, access or technical support, contact helsayed@heal-line.com.

Include your account email, the affected feature, approximate time and a brief description. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, patient information or unnecessary identity images.

Visit approvals and admission decisions belong to the destination facility. Greenmove is not an emergency service.

To report abusive content, impersonation or an inappropriate request or message, include the visit reference or the name of the other party and a description. The operator reviews reports and can restrict accounts. Reporting contact: helsayed@heal-line.com. In the app, open the visit, representative or conversation and tap Report a Concern to email us with the reference included. To stop messages from a hospital or representative, open the conversation in Messages and tap Block; Unblock restores it. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

To request permanent account deletion, sign in and open My account → Delete My Account, or see how to delete your account if you no longer have the app. The unfinished-registration screen also includes the deletion option. You will receive a deadline in the app and an email when processing is complete.

Subscription: representative accounts include a 30-day free trial, then need Greenmove Annual, a one-year auto-renewing subscription bought inside the app through Apple (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play (Android). Hospital and gate-security accounts are free. To cancel or change it on iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, open Google Play → your profile → Payments and subscriptions. The same pages open from My account in the app. If you paid but the app still asks you to subscribe, tap Restore Purchases in My account. For refunds, use https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ for Apple purchases, or request a refund from your Google Play order history.
