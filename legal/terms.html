Greenmove provides an administrative service for professional visit requests and gate coordination. You must be an adult authorized to act for the company or facility you represent. Keep account credentials private and provide accurate professional and vehicle information.

Submitting a request does not guarantee hospital participation, approval or admission. Preferred times are requests, not confirmed bookings. Follow facility instructions. Greenmove does not provide medical advice or emergency services.

Only upload information you are entitled to share. Do not submit patient data, unrelated identity documents, abusive or unlawful content, impersonation, misleading requests or malware. Report misuse through the support page. The operator may investigate and restrict accounts to protect other users and facilities, with an appropriate contact route for mistakes.

Messages in Greenmove are for professional coordination between hospital teams and representatives. Hospital teams may write to one representative or send a group message to all registered representatives, up to five group messages a day; representatives may reply. Do not send advertising unrelated to a visit, harassment or patient information. Either party can block a conversation and report it with Report a Concern, and accounts that misuse messaging can be restricted.

Subscription. Hospital and gate-security accounts are free. A new representative account includes a 30-day Greenmove trial that does not charge automatically. After the trial, Greenmove Annual is required to keep sending visit requests. Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The store that sold the subscription handles its purchases and refunds: https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ for Apple, and Google Play's refund request in your order history for Google.
