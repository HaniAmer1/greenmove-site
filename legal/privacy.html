Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Greenmove is operated by Heal Line L.L.C-FZ, United Arab Emirates. Contact: helsayed@heal-line.com. We provide professional visit coordination for medical representatives and participating hospital teams.

Information used to provide the service

We process your account name, email, mobile number, company, the names of the products you represent, professional role, specialty and city; account identifiers and authentication records; photos or business-card/ID images you choose to upload; vehicle make, model, colour and plate; visit requests, notes, responses, gate activity and notifications; and the messages you exchange with hospital teams or representatives in the app. Do not upload or send patient information or documents unrelated to professional verification.

Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not stored as readable passwords. Representative accounts include a 30-day free trial; after it, the Greenmove Annual subscription is required to keep sending visit requests. It is purchased through Apple on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play on Android. We keep the Apple or Google Play purchase identifiers and access dates needed to confirm your subscription; Apple or Google processes the payment and Greenmove never receives card numbers. Hospital and gate-security accounts are free.

The current UAE-only app does not request GPS location for country detection. It stores your chosen country on your device and uses the city and facility information you provide. Camera access is used to scan passes, and photo selection is used for images you upload. Manual pass entry remains available if you decline camera access.

Purposes and sharing

We use this information to authenticate accounts, review professional registrations, coordinate visits, verify authorized arrivals and protect the service.

Hospitals receiving your requests can access relevant profile and verification details, including your product names and, when they open your details, your mobile number. Gate staff do not receive your mobile number; they access documents through authorized pass verification or an on-site visit at their facility. Greenmove administrators review registrations. Sales managers receive assigned team information, not private verification documents through the document endpoint.

Messages: hospital teams on Greenmove can see the name and company of registered representatives so that they can write to one of them or send a group message to all of them. A conversation is visible only to that hospital's team and that representative; a group message is delivered to each representative as a private conversation. Representatives can reply, and either side can block a conversation or report it. The operator may read a reported conversation to investigate the report.

Supabase provides authentication, database and private object storage for the configured cloud service. Uploaded images are normalized, and private cloud image links expire after 60 seconds. Short-lived links do not prevent an authorized recipient from retaining a copy. Services are hosted by Supabase (https://supabase.com/privacy).

We do not sell personal information or use the current app for cross-app advertising tracking. Hosting services may process operational and network logs to deliver and secure the service.

Retention and deletion

You can request permanent account deletion inside Greenmove through My account → Delete My Account, or without the app by following the account deletion page. The option is also available while registration is unfinished. Type DELETE and submit; the app shows your deadline. We process the request within 30 days and confirm completion by email.

Processing removes the account and associated application records, including profile media, vehicle details, the user's visit records and their message conversations. If applicable law requires retaining particular records, we explain what is retained and why. Backups are used only for recovery and expire on a fixed schedule. If a backup is restored, completed deletions are applied again before the data is used.

Operational audit records are scheduled for removal after 180 days and read notifications after 90 days. Contact us to request access or correction, or for questions about data held by a hospital or payment provider.

Intended users and contact

Greenmove is for adult professionals and is not a patient-care or children's service. For privacy questions, use helsayed@heal-line.com or https://statuesque-narwhal-6ab994.netlify.app/support.html. We will update this page when our practices change.
